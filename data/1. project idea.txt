Build “GameSmith”—an AI game master that turns your room into a playable escape room.
You scan your room with your phone, choose a theme—detective mystery, sci-fi malfunction, treasure hunt—and it creates a game using objects already around you.
For example:
Your laptop displays a distress signal.
A voice says: “The clock holds the first coordinate.”
You solve a clue using its numbers.
Your phone becomes a scanner to investigate the next object.
Your friend receives a different clue—you have to cooperate.

The room becomes the game controller. Players answer, speak, scan objects, and submit photos to progress.
What makes it interesting is that the AI must create a connected, solvable game: clues depend on each other, hints respond to actual progress, and the ending follows your choices.
You’d build:
- Room understanding: identify usable objects from uploaded photos, with user confirmation.
- Puzzle generation: create structured clues and solutions, then validate their dependencies.
- Game engine: track inventory, unlocked clues, timers, and player actions.
- Live multiplayer: different clues on different devices.
- Voice and visuals: a narrator, character messages, and themed screens.
First version: one room, five confirmed objects, three players maximum, and a 15-minute mystery. Use proven puzzle templates with AI-generated stories and object mappings so the game stays solvable. Verify answers through codes and explicit actions before adding visual verification.
This is a full-stack product you can build with existing models. The engineering challenge is making the experience coherent, reliable, and fun.
Your demo is friends actually playing a game your app created from your room.
